# ABC Institute of Technology - Knowledge Base

## About the College
ABC Institute of Technology (ABCIT) is a private engineering college established in 2008. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Computer Science, Information Science, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Electronics & Communication.

## Departments
- Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
- Information Science Engineering (ISE)
- Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Civil Engineering (CE)

## Computer Science Department Overview
The CSE department focuses on software development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, and full-stack development. Students are trained in programming languages such as Python, Java, and JavaScript.

## Subjects in CSE (3rd Year)
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Operating Systems
- Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- Computer Networks
- Software Engineering

## What is DBMS?
Database Management System (DBMS) is software used to store, manage, and retrieve data efficiently. Examples include MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB.

## What is Operating System?
An Operating System (OS) is system software that manages hardware resources and provides services to applications. Examples include Linux, Windows, and macOS.

## Placement Information
The college has an average placement rate of 78%. Top recruiting companies include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, and Capgemini.

## Highest Package
The highest package offered in 2024 was 18 LPA for a Computer Science student.

## Internship Opportunities
Students are encouraged to take internships from the 2nd year onwards. The college provides support through its Training and Placement Cell.

## Library Facilities
The college library has over 50,000 books, digital resources, journals, and access to online learning platforms.

## Attendance Rules
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for exams.

## Examination System
- Internal Assessment (IA): 40 marks
- End Semester Exam: 60 marks
- Passing marks: 40%

## What is CGPA?
CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of grade points obtained in all subjects. It is used to evaluate academic performance.

## Hostel Facilities
Separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls. The hostel provides Wi-Fi, mess, and 24/7 security.

## Clubs and Activities
- Coding Club
- Robotics Club
- Cultural Club
- Entrepreneurship Cell

## Coding Club Details
The Coding Club conducts weekly coding contests, hackathons, and workshops on Data Structures, Competitive Programming, and Web Development.

## Hackathon Information
ABCIT organizes an annual 24-hour hackathon called "CodeStorm" where students build innovative projects.

## Final Year Project
Students must complete a major project in their final year. Topics include AI, Web Development, IoT, and Blockchain.

## What is Machine Learning?
Machine Learning is a field of AI where systems learn from data and improve performance without being explicitly programmed.

## What is Cloud Computing?
Cloud Computing provides on-demand computing services like storage, servers, and databases over the internet.

## Wi-Fi Access
Students can access campus Wi-Fi using their student ID and password.

## Grading System

- A+: Excellent (80-89)
- A: Very Good (70-79)
- B: Good (60-69)
- C: Average (50-59)
